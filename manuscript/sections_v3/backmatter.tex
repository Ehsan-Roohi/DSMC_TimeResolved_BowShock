\section*{Data availability}
The manuscript source, processed summary data, covariance and template-analysis scripts, figure-generation code and environment specifications are available at \url{https://github.com/Ehsan-Roohi/DSMC_TimeResolved_BowShock}. Raw DS2V snapshot files are not placed in Git because of their multi-gigabyte size; a complete manifest and instructions for reproducing the processed data from the archived raw paths are included.
