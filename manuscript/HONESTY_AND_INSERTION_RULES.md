# Control-result insertion rules

The seed and particle-number calculations are still running. The compiled V5 review draft describes the protocol and its status but **does not state that the controls passed**.

The final result paragraph may be inserted only after all four run folders pass QC, the unchanged pipeline is applied, the CSV template is filled, and no placeholder remains. A sentence such as “different seeds and particle numbers were tested and were satisfactory” is not acceptable without numerical mode alignment, memory intervals, variance ratios and full-field correlations.
