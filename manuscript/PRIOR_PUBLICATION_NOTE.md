# Prior publication and non-overlap

The 2026 Physics of Fluids article (38, 057108; DOI 10.1063/5.0334590) reported neural-operator work for rarefied cylinder flows. Its fixed-Mach-10 cross-Knudsen surrogate is described as an argon study, while its nitrogen extension varies Mach number at fixed Knudsen number. It did not publish the present time-resolved nitrogen covariance, memory, physical-support audit, detectability or displacement-template results.

The JFM manuscript now cites this paper and includes an explicit prior-publication/data-provenance statement. This is more accurate than saying that the current time-resolved N2 results were already published.
