Scientific status of draft V1
=============================

Supported central conclusions
-----------------------------
1. Corrected physical-domain field POD is high rank across the nine Knudsen numbers.
2. A weak, noise-separated collective displacement coordinate is resolved at Kn_D=0.01 and 0.025.
3. The two angular mode shapes are strongly aligned (correlation about 0.972).
4. Density and pressure provide the strongest full-field validation; Mach number and
   translational temperature supply supporting multi-moment evidence.
5. The coordinate explains only a small fraction of raw instantaneous field variance,
   so it is embedded in broadband stochastic kinetic fluctuations.
6. The current data do not establish disappearance of the coordinate at Kn_D >= 0.05,
   because detection power deteriorates rapidly.

Claims deliberately not made
-----------------------------
- No growing instability is claimed.
- No rarefaction-induced disappearance threshold is claimed.
- No new Mach sweep is included in this second paper.
- The invalid solid-side POD modes are shown only as a methodological negative control.
