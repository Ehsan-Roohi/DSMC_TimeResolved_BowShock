\section{Conclusions}
Time-resolved DSMC fields of Mach-10 nitrogen flow over a circular cylinder were analysed to distinguish a physical bow-layer displacement from correlated particle sampling fluctuations. The principal conclusions are as follows.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
\item Shock-attached interpolation must respect physical support. Solid-side nearest-neighbour filling produced false leading POD energies of order $40$--$50\%$. After gas-domain clipping, the leading combined-state energy is only $1.1$--$4.3\%$ and 146--172 modes are required for $90\%$ variance.
\item A reproducible physical angular mode is resolved at $\KnD=0.01$ and $0.025$. It is same-signed over the upstream sector, has positive far-angle covariance and retains memory $\tau_p^*=0.653$ and $0.724$.
\item The mean 10--90 density width increases by $82\%$ between the two resolved states, while the angular modes retain an absolute normalized inner product of $0.972$. The collective memory spans $6.5$--$10.6$ layer-passage times and about three standoff-passage times. Static broadening therefore occurs without immediate loss of the slow displacement geometry.
\item The coordinate is recovered independently in the complete density, pressure, Mach-number and translational-temperature fields. Density and pressure retain the strongest coherent displacement gains. As $\KnD$ increases from $0.01$ to $0.025$, the gains of Mach number and translational temperature decrease much more rapidly, and the leading four-moment synchronization fraction falls from $0.873$ to $0.706$.
\item The resulting physics is moment-selective weakening of synchrony: early rarefaction preserves the slow compression-coordinate geometry but weakens synchronous velocity--sound-speed and thermal participation. This temporal result is distinct from the mean inflation and parameter-space similarity reported in the companion arXiv paper.
\item The collective coordinate is weak, explaining only $1$--$5\%$ of raw instantaneous field variance. It is a slow coherent direction embedded in broadband kinetic fluctuations, not a dominant POD/DMD mode and not evidence of linear instability.
\item At $\KnD\geq0.05$, statistical power deteriorates too rapidly to demonstrate disappearance. The defensible result is the existence and multi-moment validation of a slow near-continuum collective coordinate, together with quantitative bounds on its identifiability.
\item Independent-seed and particle-loading controls have been pre-registered and are being acquired. Their results are not included in the present review draft and will be incorporated before submission; no successful-control claim is made in advance of those data.
\end{enumerate}
